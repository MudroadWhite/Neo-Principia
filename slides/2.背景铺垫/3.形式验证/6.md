##### 时间线3：形式验证的“正式历史”

```
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                             The Lineage of Principia Mathematica                    |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+

  1910–1913:  Whitehead & Russell — *Principia Mathematica*
      |
      |  Formal system of logic and mathematics using type theory
      |  ↓
  1940s–50s:  Early mechanical logic ideas
      |   - Alan Turing: "On Computable Numbers" (1936)
      |   - Emil Post, Alonzo Church: groundwork for formal logic automation
      |
      ↓
  1955–56:   Newell & Simon — *Logic Theorist*
      |   - First AI program to prove *PM* theorems (≈ 38 theorems from PM)
      |   - Implemented symbolic reasoning inspired by *PM*’s logic
      |
      ↓
  1958–59:   Hao Wang — Program *P*
      |   - Direct formalization of *PM* rules on IBM 704
      |   - Proved 378 theorems from *Principia Mathematica*
      |
      ↓
  1960s:     Wang’s later programs (B, C, F)
      |   - Extended to sequent calculus & predicate logic
      |   - More efficient automation of *PM*-style proofs
      |
      ↓
  1959:      Herbert Gelernter — *Geometry Theorem Prover*
      |   - Applied *PM*-like logical inference to geometry
      |
      ↓
  1960s:     McCarthy, Minsky, Davis, and Putnam
      |   - Logic-based AI systems (Advice Taker, Logic Machine)
      |   - Influenced by *PM*’s symbolic formalism
      |
      ↓
  1967–1970s:  N.G. de Bruijn — *Automath*
      |   - Fully formalized mathematics in a typed system
      |   - Described as realizing *Principia Mathematica*’s goal
      |
      ↓
  1980s–present:  Modern Proof Assistants
      |   - HOL, Isabelle/HOL → directly based on PM’s *simple type theory*
      |   - Coq, Lean → more expressive successors (type theory descendants)
      |
      ↓
  Today:  Formalized mathematics and AI theorem proving
      - Direct intellectual descendants of *Principia Mathematica*

+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```
